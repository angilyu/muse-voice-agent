Give your AI assistant a phone.

Muse Voice Agent is an MCP server that lets your AI assistant call businesses on your behalf. It books tables, gets quotes, places pickup orders, checks availability and asks questions, then reports back with a structured result.

How it works

  1. You ask your assistant. For example: "Book a table for 4 at Nopa on Friday around 7:30" or "Get quotes from two handymen to fix a leaking faucet."
  2. Your assistant confirms the brief and calls a tool. It sends the business, phone number, your name, the goal, any questions, and what the agent is allowed to agree to.
  3. The voice agent makes the call. It introduces itself as an AI assistant calling on your behalf, discloses that the call may be recorded, and handles phone menus, holds and voicemail.
  4. You get a report. Outcome, answers to each question, confirmation details, what still needs your attention, and the transcript.
  5. Callbacks are handled. If the business calls back later, the agent answers, links the call to your original request and leaves an update for your assistant.

Built-in safeguards

  • Always identifies as AI. Every call opens with "an AI assistant calling on behalf of" you, and it answers honestly if asked whether it's a person.
  • Recording disclosure. Calls start with "This call may be recorded."
  • No payment details or secrets. Card numbers and Social Security numbers are rejected before a call starts and blocked if the agent tries to say them. If a business needs payment by phone, the agent stops and asks you to follow up.
  • Only details you approve. The agent shares your name and callback number, plus anything you explicitly allow for that call. Other personal details are withheld.
  • Commits only within your limits. By default the agent only gathers information. It books or orders only when you allow it, and only within the spending, deposit, fee, time and party-size limits you set.
  • Errands only. It's for calls you ask for. It is not for telemarketing, sales, or emergency calls.

Pricing

Every new account gets 10 free minutes of calls, no card needed. After that, pick a monthly plan:

  • Starter: $5/month for 50 call minutes a month.
  • Plus: $20/month for 200 call minutes a month.

Only talk time counts: unanswered calls, callbacks from businesses and checking on a call are free. Unused minutes don't roll over. Switch plans or cancel anytime.

If you run out of minutes, your assistant tells you before any call is placed and gives you the link to subscribe. Calls already in progress are never cut off. Payments are handled by Stripe.

Connecting

Muse Voice Agent speaks the Model Context Protocol over streamable HTTP at the /mcp endpoint. Every request needs your API key as a bearer token (Authorization: Bearer <api key>). See the MCP tool reference for what each tool does. Get a key at muse-voice-agent.onrender.com/pricing.
