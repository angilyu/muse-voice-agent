Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

This policy explains what information Muse Voice Agent (the "Service") handles, why, and who it is shared with. The Service is operated by Wenjing Yu ("we", "us"). Questions or requests: angilyu@gmail.com.

What the Service does

The Service lets an AI assistant you use (an "MCP client") place phone calls to businesses on your behalf, report the results back to the assistant, and answer calls when those businesses call back. It is offered to people in the United States and Canada and only dials North American (+1) numbers.

Information we handle

Call requests from your assistant

  • The business name and phone number to call.
  • Your name, your assistant's name, and your callback number if provided.
  • What the call is for: the goal, questions, preferences, limits (such as a spending limit or date range) and any extra details you approve the agent to share, such as an email address.

Call content

  • The audio of each call, which is converted to text and spoken audio as the call happens.
  • Call recordings and transcripts. Every call starts with "This call may be recorded."
  • Call results: outcome, summary, confirmation details, answers, timing and how the call ended.

Your account and billing

  • Your email address, plan, subscription status, and how many call minutes you've used.
  • Your API key, stored only as a one-way hash, and the IP address used to sign up (to limit abuse).
  • If you subscribe, Stripe collects your payment details. We receive and store only your Stripe customer and subscription identifiers and billing status, never your card number.

Callbacks

  • When someone calls the Service's number, we receive their phone number and the call content, and link the call to your earlier request when the numbers match.

Technical information

  • Server logs with timestamps, call identifiers, phone numbers and errors, used to run and fix the Service.
  • The name your assistant's software reports when it connects (for example "muse").

What we don't collect

  • Payment card numbers, Social Security numbers and similar secrets. Requests containing them are rejected, and the agent is blocked from saying them on calls.
  • We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. The account page sets one sign-in cookie, used only to keep you signed in. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitor IP addresses under GitHub's privacy statement.

Information about the businesses we call

People who answer calls (business staff and anyone who calls the Service back) are told at the start that they're speaking with an AI assistant and that the call may be recorded. Their voice, what they say, and their phone number are processed as described in this policy. They can contact us to ask about or delete that information.

How we use information

  • To place the calls you request and say only what you approved.
  • To report results, transcripts and callbacks back to your assistant.
  • To keep the Service safe: enforcing limits, blocking sensitive data and preventing misuse.
  • To run your account: measuring call minutes, applying your plan, and billing you through Stripe.
  • To troubleshoot problems and improve call quality, including reviewing transcripts and recordings of past calls.

We don't sell personal information, share it for targeted advertising, or use it to build marketing profiles.

Who we share information with

  • The business you asked us to call: only your name, your callback number, the details of your request, and any extra details you approved for that call.
  • Your AI assistant: call status, results, transcripts and callbacks are returned to the assistant that made the request. Its handling of that information is covered by its own privacy policy.
  • Service providers that run the Service for us, each under their own terms:
    • Retell AI: voice calls, speech recognition, voice synthesis, and storing call recordings and transcripts.
    • Twilio: the phone number and the connection to the phone network.
    • OpenAI: the language model that decides what the agent says, using the call request and the conversation so far.
    • Render: hosting the server, its call database and its logs.
    • Stripe: subscriptions, payments, invoices and the billing portal.
  • When required by law, or to protect the safety of people or the Service.

How long we keep information

Call records, results and transcripts are kept until you ask us to delete them, so your assistant can look up past calls and handle callbacks. Recordings and transcripts stored with Retell AI and server logs at Render are kept under those providers' retention settings. When you ask us to delete your information, we delete it from our database and request deletion from these providers where their tools allow. Billing records kept by Stripe may be retained as required for tax and accounting.

Security

Access to the Service requires a secret API key (stored only as a hash), connections use HTTPS, calls are limited to approved number ranges, and the number of simultaneous calls is capped. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't ask the agent to share anything you wouldn't say to the business yourself.

Your choices and rights

  • You decide what the agent may share on each call and what it may agree to.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it by emailing angilyu@gmail.com. We'll respond within 30 days.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, California), you may have further rights under local law. We won't treat you differently for using them.

Children

The Service is not intended for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We'll post any changes to this policy on this page and update the effective date. If a change is significant, we'll highlight it here.
